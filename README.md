## 🌟 Why Learn Bahasa Indonesia?
- **No Grammatical Gender:** No masculine/feminine nouns.
- **No Verb Conjugation:** Verbs never change according to person or number.
- **No Tenses:** Past, present, and future are expressed simply with indicator words (*sudah*, *sedang*, *akan*).
- **Latin Alphabet:** Uses the standard 26-letter Latin alphabet with 100% phonetic pronunciation.
