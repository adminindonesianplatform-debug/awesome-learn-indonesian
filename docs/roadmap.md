# 90-Day Conversational Indonesian Learning Roadmap

A structured roadmap to achieve conversational fluency in Bahasa Indonesia in 90 days.

## Month 1: Survival & Core Pronunciation
- Day 1–7: Master basic phonetics, greetings, and numbers (1–100).
- Day 8–15: Essential travel phrases, food ordering, and taxi directions.
- Day 16–30: SVO sentence structure and high-frequency root words.

## Month 2: Daily Conversation & High-Frequency Affixes
- Day 31–45: The `me-` and `ber-` prefixes for active daily communication.
- Day 46–60: Asking questions (`kapan`, `di mana`, `kenapa`, `bagaimana`).

## Month 3: Indonesian Slang & Real-World Immersion
- Day 61–75: Informal colloquial Indonesian (*Bahasa Gaul*) used by locals.
- Day 76–90: Full conversational practice with native tutors.
