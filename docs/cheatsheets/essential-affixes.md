# Indonesian Affixes Master Cheat Sheet (Imbuhan)

> Practical breakdown of prefixes and suffixes in Bahasa Indonesia.  
> Originally curated by [Learn Indonesian Easy Way](https://www.learnindonesianeasyway.com).

Bahasa Indonesia uses affixes (*imbuhan*) added to root words (*kata dasar*) to change their meaning and grammatical function.

---

## 1. Prefix `me-` / `men-` / `meng-` / `mem-` (Active Verb)
Indicates an active action performed by the subject.

| Root Word | With Prefix | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **baca** (read) | **membaca** | to read | *Saya membaca buku.* (I am reading a book) |
| **tulis** (write) | **menulis** | to write | *Dia menulis pesan.* (She writes a message) |
| **lihat** (see) | **melihat** | to see / look | *Kami melihat pemandangan.* (We see the view) |
| **makan** (eat) | **memakan** | to consume | *Kucing memakan ikan.* (The cat consumes the fish) |
| **beli** (buy) | **membeli** | to buy | *Saya membeli kopi.* (I buy coffee) |

---

## 2. Prefix `di-` (Passive Verb)
Turns an action into passive voice (the object receives the action).

| Active | Passive | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Saya membaca buku* | *Buku **dibaca** oleh saya* | The book is read by me |
| *Dia membeli kopi* | *Kopi **dibeli** oleh dia* | The coffee is bought by her |
| *Mereka menjual rumah* | *Rumah **dijual*** | The house is for sale / sold |

---

## 3. Prefix `ber-` (State, Possession, Intransitive)
Indicates doing an action for oneself, possessing something, or being in a state.

| Root Word | With `ber-` | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **jalan** (road) | **berjalan** | to walk | *Saya berjalan ke kantor.* |
| **bicara** (speech) | **berbicara** | to speak | *Bisa berbicara bahasa Inggris?* |
| **pikir** (thought) | **berpikir** | to think | *Saya sedang berpikir.* |
| **kumpul** (gather) | **berkumpul** | to gather together | *Keluarga berkumpul di Bali.* |
