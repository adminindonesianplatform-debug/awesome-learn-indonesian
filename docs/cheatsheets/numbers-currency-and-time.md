# Indonesian Numbers, Rupiah Currency & Telling Time

> Complete cheat sheet for handling Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) and daily schedules.  
> Curated by [Learn Indonesian Easy Way](https://www.learnindonesianeasyway.com).

---

## 1. Numbers (1 to 10)
* 1 — **Satu**
* 2 — **Dua**
* 3 — **Tiga**
* 4 — **Empat**
* 5 — **Lima**
* 6 — **Enam**
* 7 — **Tujuh**
* 8 — **Delapan**
* 9 — **Sembilan**
* 10 — **Sepuluh**

---

## 2. Currency Multipliers (Essential for IDR)
* **Belas** — -teen (*sebelas* = 11, *dua belas* = 12)
* **Puluh** — -ty (*dua puluh* = 20, *lima puluh* = 50)
* **Ratus** — Hundred (*seratus* = 100, *dua ratus* = 200)
* **Ribu** — Thousand (*sepuluh ribu* = 10,000 IDR ~ $0.65 USD)
* **Juta** — Million (*satu juta* = 1,000,000 IDR ~ $65 USD)

---

## 3. Days of the Week
* Monday — **Senin**
* Tuesday — **Selasa**
* Wednesday — **Rabu**
* Thursday — **Kamis**
* Friday — **Jumat**
* Saturday — **Sabtu**
* Sunday — **Minggu**
