# Indonesian Travel Survival Phrases Cheat Sheet

> Essential phrases for navigating Bali, Jakarta, Yogyakarta, and Lombok.  
> Source: [Learn Indonesian Easy Way — Travel Guide](https://www.learnindonesianeasyway.com/blog/indonesian-phrases-for-travel).

---

## 1. Essential Greetings & Politeness
* **Terima kasih** — Thank you
* **Sama-sama** — You're welcome
* **Tolong** — Please (when asking for help)
* **Permisi** — Excuse me (when passing someone or getting attention)
* **Maaf** — Sorry / Pardon
* **Selamat pagi** — Good morning (6 AM – 11 AM)
* **Selamat siang** — Good afternoon (11 AM – 3 PM)
* **Selamat sore** — Good late afternoon (3 PM – 6 PM)
* **Selamat malam** — Good evening / Good night

---

## 2. Getting Around & Transport
* **Berapa harganya?** — How much does this cost?
* **Bisa kurang?** — Can you discount it? (Friendly bargaining)
* **Mau ke mana?** — Where are you going? (Friendly greeting in Indonesia)
* **Saya mau ke [tempat]** — I want to go to [place]
* **Belok kiri / Belok kanan** — Turn left / Turn right
* **Lurus terus** — Go straight ahead
* **Berhenti di sini, ya** — Stop here, please

---

## 3. Ordering Food & Drinks
* **Minta menu, ya** — May I see the menu?
* **Saya pesan ini satu** — I would like to order one of this
* **Tidak pedas / Kurang pedas** — Not spicy / Less spicy
* **Tanpa gula** — Without sugar (vital for iced tea/coffee)
* **Bungkus** — Takeaway / To go
* **Berapa semuanya?** — How much is everything?
* **Enak sekali!** — Delicious!
