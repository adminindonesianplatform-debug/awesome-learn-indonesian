# Indonesian Colloquial Slang (Bahasa Gaul) Guide

> How Indonesians actually speak in daily life and text messaging.  
> Curated by [Learn Indonesian Easy Way](https://www.learnindonesianeasyway.com).

Formal textbook Indonesian is very different from daily street Indonesian (*Bahasa Gaul*). Here are the most common informal words you will hear everywhere.

---

## 1. Casual Pronouns
* **Gue / Gua** — I / Me (Informal, Jakarta slang used nationwide)
* **Lu / Lo** — You (Casual between friends)
* **Kita** — We (all of us)
* **Mereka** — They

---

## 2. High-Frequency Slang Words
| Slang | Formal Equivalent | Meaning & Context | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Banget** | Sangat / Sekali | Very / Extremely | *Enak banget!* (So delicious!) |
| **Gimana** | Bagaimana | How | *Gimana kabarnya?* (How are you doing?) |
| **Udah** | Sudah | Already | *Udah makan belum?* (Have you eaten yet?) |
| **Nggak / Gak** | Tidak | No / Not | *Gak apa-apa.* (No problem / It's okay) |
| **Nongkrong** | Berkumpul santai | Hang out with friends | *Yuk nongkrong di kafe!* |
| **Santai** | Tenang | Relaxed / Chill | *Santai aja bro.* (Just relax) |
| **Bisa kali** | Mungkin bisa | Could be possible / Why not | *Ikut kita, bisa kali!* |
